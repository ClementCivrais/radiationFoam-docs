radiationFoam
=============

radiationFoam computes the spectral emission, absorption and transport of
radiation in high-temperature, non-equilibrium gases, and reproduces what a
shock-tube spectrometer records. It is an OpenFOAM v2512 application built on
one library, ``libradiationFoam``, and needs no other library.

The populations of the radiating states come from either of two inputs:

* **temperatures**, re-expanded into Boltzmann populations over the
  rovibronic levels of each species; or
* **the local state distribution** :math:`n(n,v,J)` of each cell, sampled
  by the rovibronic DSMC solver dsmcFoam+, with no assumption on the shape
  of the distribution.

The second input is the purpose of the code: the radiance is computed
directly from the state-resolved populations of the gas kinetics, with the
same spectroscopic database on both sides.
