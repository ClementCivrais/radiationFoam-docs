About
=====

What radiationFoam is
---------------------

radiationFoam computes the spectral emission, absorption and radiative
transfer of high-temperature gases in OpenFOAM, line by line, from the
level populations of each cell. The radiators of the present database are
the air species N, O, N+, O+ (atomic lines) and N2, N2+, NO, O2 (diatomic
band systems), with bound-free and free-free continua for the atoms and
atomic ions. The populations come either from temperatures (a Boltzmann
re-expansion over the levels of each species) or from the state-resolved
level distribution of each cell, sampled by a DSMC simulation, with no
assumption on its shape.

The code computes what a shock-tube spectrometer records: the radiance
across the tube, band integrals, and the measured spectra and band
profiles after the instrument line shape and spatial resolution of the
NASA EAST facility (Brandis and Cruden 2017). Its goal is the comparison of
DSMC flow fields with shock-tube spectra and, in a later stage, the
radiative heating of entry flows (:doc:`/guide/dsmcfoam_coupling`).

It consists of

* ``libradiationFoam`` (``src/radiation``): radiators, line shapes,
  population models, continuum processes, transport modes and the
  instrument model (:doc:`/guide/architecture`);
* ``radiationFoam`` (``applications/solvers/radiationFoam``): the
  application;
* ``Test-radiationInstrument`` (``applications/test/radiationInstrument``):
  the test driver of the instrument model;
* ``src/radiation/database``: the spectroscopic database, every value
  referenced in ``SOURCES.md`` and generated by the scripts of
  ``sources/``.

It is built against OpenFOAM v2512 (openfoam.com) only
(:doc:`/guide/installation`).

Relation to dsmcFoam+
---------------------

radiationFoam is the radiation counterpart of the rovibronic DSMC solver
developed on top of dsmcFoam+ (White et al. 2018), in which every simulator
particle carries its electronic, vibrational and rotational quantum numbers
:math:`(n,v,J)` (Civrais et al. 2024; Civrais 2024). That solver's
documentation is at https://clementcivrais.github.io/DSMC_Rovibronic-docs/.

The two codes share the spectroscopic levels: the populations sampled per
level by dsmcFoam are written by the function object
``dsmcRadiationDistribution`` of the DSMC plug-in
``libdsmcRadiationCoupling`` and read by radiationFoam's
``cellDistribution`` population model. radiationFoam itself needs no DSMC
library. The library was developed inside the DSMC repository
(``src/lagrangian/DSMC/radiation``) and moved to its own repository with
its history on 2026-11-16.

How to cite
-----------

There is no journal article on radiationFoam yet. Until there is, please
cite the software through its repository, and the rovibronic DSMC work it
builds on:

* Civrais, C. H. B. radiationFoam: spectral radiation from state-resolved
  populations in OpenFOAM. Source code, https://github.com/ClementCivrais/radiationFoam.
* Civrais, C. H. B., Pfeiffer, M., White, C. and Steijl, R. (2024).
  Modelling of the electronic excited states in high-temperature flows.
  Physics of Fluids, 36(8), 086112. doi:10.1063/5.0215853.

When the EAST instrument model is used, cite the source of the instrument
functions and data, Brandis and Cruden (2017).
