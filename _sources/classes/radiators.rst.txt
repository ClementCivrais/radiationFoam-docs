Radiating species
=================

Source: ``src/radiation/radiators/``.

A radiating species computes the spectral emission
:math:`j_\lambda` [W m\ :sup:`-3` sr\ :sup:`-1` m\ :sup:`-1`] and absorption
:math:`\kappa_\lambda` [m\ :sup:`-1`] of one species in one cell. The base
class is run-time selected; two derived classes exist::

    radiators/
        basic/radiatingSpecies/     abstract base, selector (entry model)
        derived/atomicLines/        atoms and atomic ions, line by line
        derived/diatomicBands/      diatomic molecules and ions, band systems
                                    line by line (three source files)

Theory: :doc:`/theory/atomic_lines`, :doc:`/theory/diatomic_bands`.

radiatingSpecies (base class)
-----------------------------

``src/radiation/radiators/basic/radiatingSpecies/radiatingSpecies{,New}.{H,C}``

One sub-dictionary per species in the ``species`` dictionary of
``system/radiationProperties``, keyed by the species name. The selector
reads ``model``.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 22 12 14 52

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
   * - ``model``
     - word
     - required
     - ``atomicLines`` or ``diatomicBands``
   * - ``mass``
     - scalar
     - required
     - particle mass [kg]
   * - ``radius``
     - scalar
     - required
     - hard-sphere radius [m] (phase broadening of atomic lines; unused by
       ``diatomicBands``)
   * - ``<model>Coeffs``
     - dictionary
     - required
     - coefficients of the derived class

The entries ``windowEdge`` and ``subBinLines`` were removed: a case that
gives either (in the species dictionary or an enclosing one) stops with a
FatalIOError. The line placement has one form: every grid point receives
the line profile integral over its own bin, at the window edges and for
lines narrower than a bin (:doc:`/theory/line_shapes`).

Interface:

* ``name()``, ``mass()``, ``radius()``;
* ``levels()``: the ``radiationLevels`` whose populations the species needs
  (:doc:`common`);
* ``addEmissionAbsorption(grid, populations, state, speciesi, emission,
  absorption)``: add the species' :math:`j_\lambda` and
  :math:`\kappa_\lambda` on the grid, with the populations of the
  ``radiationPopulationModel`` (:doc:`populations`); ``speciesi`` is the
  index of the species in the cell state.

The species does not compute populations; it passes its levels to the
population model and receives :math:`n_i`, the bound limit and the
electron temperature for the Stark width.

atomicLines
-----------

``src/radiation/radiators/derived/atomicLines/atomicLines.{H,C}``

Bound-bound emission and absorption of an atom or atomic ion from a list
of levels and lines in the atomic format of the database
(:doc:`database`). At construction it reads the transition file and
prints the number of levels and lines.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 22 12 14 52

   * - Keyword (``atomicLinesCoeffs``)
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
   * - ``transitionFile``
     - fileName
     - required
     - atomic file (environment variables expanded)
   * - ``ionisationEnergy``
     - scalar
     - required
     - ionisation energy [cm\ :sup:`-1`]; with the lowering it gives the
       bound limit. Must be the real limit: with 0 no line is computed
   * - ``starkExponent``
     - scalar
     - required
     - temperature exponent :math:`n_S` of the Stark width [-]
   * - ``lineShape``
     - word
     - required
     - line profile model (``voigtWhitingOliveroLongbothum``,
       :doc:`line_shapes`)

The entry ``starkDensity`` was removed (electron-impact Stark broadening
with :math:`n_e` and :math:`T_e` of the cell is the only form); a case that
gives it stops with a FatalIOError.

.. code-block:: cpp

   N
   {
       model           atomicLines;
       mass            2.326e-26;      // [kg]
       radius          0.7e-10;        // [m]

       atomicLinesCoeffs
       {
           transitionFile      "$RADIATIONFOAM_DIR/src/radiation/database/atoms/N_I.dat";
           ionisationEnergy    117225.7;       // [cm^-1], NIST ASD v5.12
           starkExponent       0.33;           // Johnston (2006) Eq. 3.13
           lineShape           voigtWhitingOliveroLongbothum;
       }
   }

Per cell (``addEmissionAbsorption``):

1. skip the species if :math:`n < 10` m\ :sup:`-3` or
   :math:`T_{\mathrm{trans}} < 10` K, or if the population model returns no
   population;
2. compute the decay rate :math:`\Gamma` of each level, the mixture density
   and the collision rate for the phase width (radiating species only);
3. per line: skip if the upper level is above the bound limit; compute the
   integrated emission and absorption and the five Lorentzian widths and
   the Doppler width; skip if farther than
   :math:`100\max(w_L, w_D) + \Delta\lambda` from the grid; get the
   bin-integrated profile from the line-shape model and add
   :math:`\max(0,\epsilon)\phi_i` to the emission and :math:`k\,\phi_i`
   (signed, gain kept) to the absorption;
4. with a population model that models levels (``cellDistribution``
   ``boltzmannFill``), report the emission of the lines with a modelled
   upper level (``recordModelledEmission``).

Members: ``nLevels()``, ``nLines()``, ``levels()``. Constants: exact SI 2019
:math:`k_B, h, c, e`.

diatomicBands
-------------

``src/radiation/radiators/derived/diatomicBands/``: ``diatomicBands.H``,
``diatomicBands.C`` (constructor, per-cell coefficients),
``diatomicBandsRead.C`` (molecule file), ``diatomicBandsLines.C`` (line
positions, Hoenl-London factors, levels).

Bound-bound emission and absorption of a diatomic molecule or diatomic
ion from the band systems of a molecule file of the database
(:doc:`database`). Every ``[band-...]`` block of the file is computed;
there is no band selection.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 22 12 14 52

   * - Keyword (``diatomicBandsCoeffs``)
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
   * - ``transitionFile``
     - fileName
     - required
     - molecule file (environment variables expanded)

.. code-block:: cpp

   N2
   {
       model           diatomicBands;
       mass            4.652e-26;      // [kg]
       radius          0;              // unused by this class

       diatomicBandsCoeffs
       {
           transitionFile  "$RADIATIONFOAM_DIR/src/radiation/database/molecules/N2.dat";
       }
   }

Construction (once):

* ``readParade`` reads the states, bands, 17 constants per state and the
  transition rows (records starting with ``c`` are comments; exactly one
  record is skipped after each row count, item 1 of the header); it
  converts energies from cm\ :sup:`-1` to J and :math:`r_e` from cm to m;
* the log lists every band (upper and lower state, type, rows, rows not
  read);
* ``buildLines`` builds, per row :math:`(v', v'')`, the band origin, the
  band strength :math:`c_S` and every rotational line of the transition
  type (1 to 6; another type is a fatal error): wavenumber, emission
  factor per unit :math:`n_u/g_u`, absorption factor per unit
  :math:`n_l/g_l - n_u/g_u`, rotational term difference
  :math:`F' - F''`, and the indices of its upper and lower level; it
  creates the levels (key, :math:`g`, energy parts) and copies the state
  constants into the ``radiationLevels``;
* the log gives the number of vibrational transitions, lines and levels.

Fatal errors at construction: a band referring to a state outside
``[Energy-Levels]``, a type-3 band with ``altnat'`` :math:`\neq 0`, a type
4-6 band with :math:`k_{\max} > 500`, the two states of a type 3/5/6 band
with different spins, a spin :math:`S > 1` in the matrix method, a level
reached with two different energies, a level outside the key range.

Per cell (``addEmissionAbsorption``):

1. skip if :math:`n < 10` m\ :sup:`-3` or :math:`T_{\mathrm{trans}} < 10` K,
   or if the population model returns no population;
2. compute the foreign-gas density and molar mass;
3. per vibrational transition: skip if outside the window (origin test);
   compute the Doppler, self, foreign, natural and Stark widths at the
   band origin, the Voigt width and the 2001-point cumulative Whiting
   table over :math:`\pm 25` Voigt widths;
4. per line: :math:`\epsilon = ` emission factor :math:`\times n_u/g_u`;
   decide whether it is placed (``emissionSelectsLines`` of the population
   model, threshold :math:`10^{-25}` in the code's units); get
   :math:`n_l/g_l` from ``lineLowerPopulation``; place the bin integrals of
   the cumulative profile on the grid; add :math:`\epsilon/10^{10}\,\phi_i`
   and :math:`k/10^{10}\,\phi_i` (signed);
5. as ``atomicLines``, report modelled-level emission when the population
   model models levels.

Members: ``nLines()``, ``levels()``. Constants: exact SI 2019
:math:`k_B, h, c`, CODATA 2018 atomic mass unit,
:math:`e a_0 = 2.5415785\times10^{-18}` esu cm.

The header ``diatomicBands.H`` lists, as items 1-13, the forms of the
verified version that are kept on purpose (reader, :math:`k_{\max}`,
type-specific terms, widths); they are summarised in
:doc:`/theory/diatomic_bands`.
