Input Reference
===============

The present page lists every keyword that radiationFoam and
``libradiationFoam`` read, grouped by dictionary and model. The list was
built from the parsers themselves (every ``get``, ``getOrDefault``,
``readIfPresent``, ``found``, ``lookup``, ``subDict`` and ``findDict`` call
in ``src/radiation`` and ``applications``), so it documents what the code
reads, not an intended interface. "required" in the *Default* column means
that a missing entry stops the run with a FatalIOError.

Unless a row says otherwise, quantities are SI (m, m\ :sup:`-3`, K, kg,
s). The exceptions are the spectral grid and band bounds (nm), the atomic
ionisation energy (cm\ :sup:`-1`) and the lengths of the instrument
dictionaries (``lengthUnit``, ``bandUnit``, ``distanceUnit``).

The physics behind each keyword is explained in the
:doc:`/theory/index`; the classes that read them in
:doc:`/classes/index`.

Overview
--------

Everything is read from ``system/radiationProperties``:

.. code-block:: text

   radiationProperties
   ├── mode                          singleCell | shockTube1D
   ├── populationModel               boltzmannTemperatures | cellDistribution
   ├── <populationModel>Coeffs       optional
   ├── continuum                     none | physical          (optional)
   ├── species
   │   └── <s>                       one per radiating species
   │       ├── model, mass, radius
   │       ├── <model>Coeffs         atomicLinesCoeffs | diatomicBandsCoeffs
   │       ├── continuum             optional per-species override
   │       └── continuumCoeffs       continuum physical only
   ├── spectralGrid                  singleCell
   ├── cellState                     singleCell
   └── shockTube1DCoeffs             shockTube1D
       ├── diameter, windows, bands, profile
       ├── distribution              optional (cellDistribution)
       ├── writeSpectra, writeCoefficients, writeDistributions
       └── instrument                optional
           └── <camera>
               ├── window, wavelengthRange, bandUnit, bands
               ├── spectralResponse, spatialResponse   (instrument functions)
               ├── shockFront, distanceUnit, metricHalfWidth, spatialBinWidth
               └── spectra, writeField

Two other inputs are files with their own format: the level-distribution
files (`Level-distribution file`_) and the macro file of a 1-D profile
(`Macro file`_). The data files of the database (transition files,
photoionisation files) are described in :doc:`/classes/database`.

``system/controlDict`` is the standard OpenFOAM file (see
:doc:`running`); radiationFoam reads no keyword of its own there.

Top level of ``radiationProperties``
------------------------------------

Radiating species
-----------------

Common entries: ``species/<s>``
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Read by the base class ``radiatingSpecies``
(``radiators/basic/radiatingSpecies/radiatingSpecies.C`` and
``radiatingSpeciesNew.C``) for every species.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``model``
     - word
     - required
     - Radiator class: ``atomicLines`` (atoms and atomic ions) or
       ``diatomicBands`` (diatomic molecules and ions).
     - ``radiatingSpeciesNew.C``
   * - ``mass``
     - scalar [kg]
     - required
     - Particle mass: Doppler width and the mass density of the
       perturbers.
     - ``radiatingSpecies.C``
   * - ``radius``
     - scalar [m]
     - required
     - Hard-sphere radius, used in the phase-interrupting (collision)
       broadening of the atomic lines, both for the radiator and for every
       radiating species of the cell as a perturber,
       :math:`\nu_\mathrm{coll}\propto\sum_j n_j\pi(r+r_j)^2`. Read for every
       species; the lines of ``diatomicBands`` do not use it (the tutorials
       give 0 for N2).
     - ``radiatingSpecies.C``
   * - ``<model>Coeffs``
     - dict
     - required
     - ``atomicLinesCoeffs`` or ``diatomicBandsCoeffs`` (``subDict``, so
       required even when the class reads few entries).
     - ``radiatingSpecies.C``
   * - ``continuum``
     - word
     - top-level ``continuum``
     - Per-species override of the continuum: ``none`` or ``physical``.
       Given on a species whose model is not ``atomicLines`` with a value
       other than ``none``: FatalIOError.
     - ``continuumProcesses.C``
   * - ``continuumCoeffs``
     - dict
     - empty
     - Continuum coefficients (`Continuum`_).
     - ``continuumModel.C``

``atomicLinesCoeffs``
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Bound-bound lines of an atom or atomic ion
(``radiators/derived/atomicLines/atomicLines.C``; theory in
:doc:`/theory/atomic_lines`).

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``transitionFile``
     - fileName
     - required
     - Atomic transition file of the database (levels and lines), e.g.
       ``"$RADIATIONFOAM_DIR/src/radiation/database/atoms/N_I.dat"``.
       Environment variables and ``<case>`` are expanded; the path is
       opened as written.
     - ``atomicLines.C``
   * - ``ionisationEnergy``
     - scalar [cm\ :sup:`-1`]
     - required
     - Ionisation limit of the species, converted to J by
       :math:`hc\times 100`. Upper bound of the bound levels, lowered by
       the Debye-Hückel term. The tutorials use the NIST ASD v5.12 values
       (N I 117225.7, O I 109837.02).
     - ``atomicLines.C``
   * - ``starkExponent``
     - scalar [-]
     - required
     - Exponent :math:`n` of the electron-temperature dependence of the
       Stark width,
       :math:`\Delta\lambda_S=\Delta\lambda_{S,0}(T_e/10^4\,\mathrm{K})^n
       (n_e/10^{22}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}})`. The tutorials use 0.33 (1/3,
       Johnston 2006, Eq. 3.13).
     - ``atomicLines.C``
   * - ``lineShape``
     - word
     - required
     - Line-shape model, run-time selected. Only
       ``voigtWhitingOliveroLongbothum`` exists (Voigt width of Liu et
       al. 2001, Whiting 1968 shape, integrated per bin). The model
       reads no other entry.
     - ``lineShapes/basic/lineShapeModel/lineShapeModelNew.C``

``diatomicBandsCoeffs``
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Bound-bound band systems of a diatomic, line by line
(``radiators/derived/diatomicBands/diatomicBands.C``; theory in
:doc:`/theory/diatomic_bands`).

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``transitionFile``
     - fileName
     - required
     - Molecule file of the database (electronic states, band systems,
       transition tables), e.g.
       ``"$RADIATIONFOAM_DIR/src/radiation/database/molecules/N2.dat"``.
       Every band system not commented out in the file is computed (no
       band selection keyword).
     - ``diatomicBands.C``

Population models
-----------------

``boltzmannTemperatures`` reads no entry
(``populations/derived/boltzmannTemperatures``). It needs, per species,
the electronic temperature, and for diatomics the vibrational and
rotational temperatures, from the cell state.

``cellDistributionCoeffs`` (all optional,
``populations/derived/cellDistribution/cellDistribution.C``; theory in
:doc:`/theory/populations`):

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``missingLevels``
     - word
     - ``zero``
     - Levels of the spectroscopic data covered by no entry of the
       distribution: ``zero`` (population 0, reported in the log) or
       ``boltzmannFill`` (Boltzmann population at the species electronic
       temperature of the cell state; levels the DSMC ladder carries but
       that received no parcel keep 0 when the file has a ``coverage``
       entry). Any other value: FatalIOError.
     - ``cellDistribution.C``
   * - ``fillReport``
     - fileName
     - ``"<case>/postProcessing/radiationFoam/cellDistributionFill.csv"``
     - CSV file of the ``boltzmannFill`` diagnostics: one row per call
       (cell, species, window), with the filled share of the emission.
       Expanded; relative to the case.
     - ``cellDistribution.C``

The level populations of ``cellDistribution`` come from the cell state:
``cellState/distributionFile`` (``singleCell``) or
``shockTube1DCoeffs/distribution`` (``shockTube1D``).

Continuum
---------

The continuum is built by ``continuumProcesses``
(``continuum/continuumProcesses/continuumProcesses.C``). With
``physical``, for every species of model ``atomicLines``:

* a ``boundFreePhysical`` process if ``continuumCoeffs`` gives
  ``ionSpecies`` (else none, reported in the log);
* a ``freeFreePhysical`` process if ``chargeNumber`` :math:`\geq 1`.

Species of another model are skipped by the global entry. Theory in
:doc:`/theory/continuum`.

``continuumCoeffs`` (in ``species/<s>``):

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``chargeNumber``
     - label
     - required (continuum physical)
     - Charge of the species: 0 for N, O; 1 for N+, O+.
     - ``continuumProcesses.C``, ``continuumModel.C``
   * - ``ionSpecies``
     - word
     - none
     - The radiating species one charge higher (the recombining ion of the
       bound-free process). Must be a species of the case and not the
       species itself (FatalIOError).
     - ``continuumModel.C``
   * - ``crossSection``
     - word
     - required (bound-free)
     - Photoionisation cross sections: ``hydrogenic`` (Kramers, with the
       Gaunt factor below) or ``tabulated`` (file below).
     - ``continuum/derived/boundFreePhysical/boundFreePhysical.C``
   * - ``photoionisationFile``
     - fileName
     - required (``tabulated``)
     - Photoionisation file of the database, e.g.
       ``"$RADIATIONFOAM_DIR/src/radiation/database/atoms/N_I_photoionisation.dat"``
       (one row per level of the transition file).
     - ``boundFreePhysical.C``
   * - ``gauntFactor``
     - scalar
     - 1
     - Gaunt factor of the hydrogenic bound-free cross section.
     - ``boundFreePhysical.C``
   * - ``gauntFreeFree``
     - scalar
     - 1
     - Free-free Gaunt factor (1: Kramers).
     - ``continuum/derived/freeFreePhysical/freeFreePhysical.C``

Example (atomic nitrogen with its ion):

.. code-block:: c++

   continuum       physical;

   species
   {
       N
       {
           model           atomicLines;
           ...
           continuumCoeffs
           {
               chargeNumber        0;
               ionSpecies          N+;
               crossSection        tabulated;
               photoionisationFile "$RADIATIONFOAM_DIR/src/radiation/database/atoms/N_I_photoionisation.dat";
           }
       }
       N+
       {
           model           atomicLines;
           ...
           continuumCoeffs { chargeNumber 1; }
       }
   }

Mode ``singleCell``
-------------------

``transport/derived/singleCell/singleCell.C``: one cell, the emission and
absorption coefficients on one grid, no transfer, no mesh.

``spectralGrid``
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Also the format of every window of ``shockTube1DCoeffs/windows``
(``common/spectralGrid/spectralGrid.C``). The grid is uniform:
:math:`\lambda_i=\lambda_\mathrm{min}+i\,\Delta\lambda`,
:math:`\Delta\lambda=(\lambda_\mathrm{max}-\lambda_\mathrm{min})/(N-1)`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``minWavelength``
     - scalar [nm]
     - required
     - :math:`\lambda_\mathrm{min}` (vacuum wavelength).
     - ``spectralGrid.C``
   * - ``maxWavelength``
     - scalar [nm]
     - required
     - :math:`\lambda_\mathrm{max}`; must exceed ``minWavelength``.
     - ``spectralGrid.C``
   * - ``nPoints``
     - label
     - required
     - Number of grid points :math:`N`, at least 100 (FatalIOError
       below).
     - ``spectralGrid.C``

``cellState``
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``electronNumberDensity``
     - scalar [m\ :sup:`-3`]
     - required
     - :math:`n_e` of the cell (Stark width, Debye-Hückel lowering,
       continuum).
     - ``singleCell.C``
   * - ``electronTemperature``
     - scalar [K]
     - required
     - :math:`T_e` of the cell. :math:`T_e\leq 0`: the line widths use the
       temperature of the population model instead; no continuum.
     - ``singleCell.C``
   * - ``distributionFile``
     - fileName
     - none
     - Level-distribution file of the cell (`Level-distribution file`_),
       read when given; needed by ``populationModel cellDistribution``.
       Expanded; relative to the case.
     - ``singleCell.C``
   * - ``species``
     - dict
     - required
     - One sub-dictionary per radiating species (all species of
       ``species`` must appear).
     - ``singleCell.C``

``cellState/species/<s>``:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``numberDensity``
     - scalar [m\ :sup:`-3`]
     - required
     - Number density of the species.
     - ``singleCell.C``
   * - ``translationalTemperature``
     - scalar [K]
     - required
     - Translational temperature (Doppler and pressure widths).
     - ``singleCell.C``
   * - ``electronicTemperature``
     - scalar [K]
     - 0 (warning)
     - Electronic temperature of the Boltzmann re-expansion. Absent: 0,
       i.e. no electronic excitation (the species in its lowest
       electronic level(s)); a warning is printed.
     - ``singleCell.C``
   * - ``vibrationalTemperature``
     - scalar [K]
     - 0
     - Diatomics: temperature of the vibrational factor of the Boltzmann
       re-expansion. No population below 10 K (warning).
     - ``singleCell.C``
   * - ``rotationalTemperature``
     - scalar [K]
     - 0
     - Diatomics: temperature of the rotational factor. Must be positive
       for a diatomic with ``boltzmannTemperatures``.
     - ``singleCell.C``

Example (``tutorials/singleCell_N``):

.. code-block:: c++

   mode            singleCell;
   populationModel boltzmannTemperatures;

   spectralGrid
   {
       minWavelength       867.9;     // [nm]
       maxWavelength       869.2;     // [nm]
       nPoints             1000;
   }

   cellState
   {
       electronNumberDensity   1e21;           // [m^-3]
       electronTemperature     10000;          // [K]

       species
       {
           N
           {
               numberDensity               1e21;   // [m^-3]
               translationalTemperature    20000;  // [K]
               electronicTemperature       10000;  // [K]
           }
       }
   }

Level-distribution file
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Read by ``radiationCellState::readDistribution``
(``common/radiationCellState/radiationCellState.C``), for
``cellState/distributionFile`` and for every ``cell<k>`` file of
``shockTube1DCoeffs/distribution``. It is an OpenFOAM dictionary, the
format written by the dsmcFoam function object
``dsmcRadiationDistribution`` (:doc:`dsmcfoam_coupling`):

.. code-block:: c++

   cell    12;                     // optional, -1 if absent (fill report)
   N
   {
       levels
       (
           (1)        9.6e20       // atom: one level label of the transition file
           (6 7 8)    3.1e15       // atom: a group of level labels
       );
       g        ( 4 36 );          // optional: DSMC level weight per entry
       coverage complete;          // optional: every carried level is an entry
   }
   N2
   {
       levels
       (
           (X 0 12)   1.2e18       // diatomic: (electronic state, v, J)
           ("B'" 1 3) 4.0e12       // quoted when not a valid word
       );
       coverage ( (X 0 0 98) (X 1 0 97) );   // optional: runs (state v J0 J1) carried
   }

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``cell``
     - label
     - -1
     - Cell index, copied to the fill report.
     - ``radiationCellState.C``
   * - ``<s>``
     - dict
     - none
     - One sub-dictionary per species. A species absent from the file has
       no distribution (fatal for ``cellDistribution``); an empty
       ``levels ( );`` is a distribution with no populated level.
     - ``radiationCellState.C``
   * - ``<s>/levels``
     - list
     - required in ``<s>``
     - Entries ``(identifiers) numberDensity`` [m\ :sup:`-3`].
       Identifiers are words, integers or quoted strings. Atom: level
       labels; diatomic: exactly (state, v, J).
     - ``radiationCellState.C``
   * - ``<s>/g``
     - scalarList
     - none
     - Statistical weight of the DSMC level of each entry; must have the
       length of ``levels`` (FatalIOError otherwise).
     - ``radiationCellState.C``
   * - ``<s>/coverage``
     - ``complete`` or list
     - none
     - Which DSMC levels the averaging block carried: ``complete``
       (atoms) or runs ``(state v J0 J1)`` (diatomics). Used by
       ``missingLevels boltzmannFill``.
     - ``radiationCellState.C``

The other entries written by ``dsmcRadiationDistribution``
(``centre``, ``volume``, ``averagingTime``, ``nSamples``,
``numberDensity``, ``parcelSamples``, ``parcelCounts``, ``energy``) are
not read.

Mode ``shockTube1D``
--------------------

``transport/derived/shockTube1D/shockTube1D.C``: the cells of a 1-D
profile, each an independent uniform slab of thickness :math:`D` (the tube
diameter), with radiance
:math:`I_\lambda=-(j_\lambda/\kappa_\lambda)\,\mathrm{expm1}(-\kappa_\lambda D)`
(:math:`j_\lambda D` when :math:`\kappa_\lambda=0`). Theory in
:doc:`/theory/transport`.

``shockTube1DCoeffs``
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``diameter``
     - scalar [m]
     - required
     - Slab thickness :math:`D`, the tube diameter (EAST: 0.1016).
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``windows``
     - dict
     - required
     - One sub-dictionary per spectral window, keyword = window name,
       each a `spectralGrid`_ (``minWavelength``, ``maxWavelength``,
       ``nPoints``).
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``bands``
     - dict
     - required
     - Raw band integrals: ``name (a b);`` with :math:`a<b` in nm. A band
       must lie inside one window (FatalIOError otherwise); a grid point on
       a band edge is inside the band. May be empty (``bands { }``).
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``profile``
     - dict
     - required
     - The cell states (`profile`_).
     - ``profiles/shockTubeProfile/shockTubeProfile.C``
   * - ``distribution``
     - dict
     - none
     - Level distributions of the cells (`distribution`_), for
       ``populationModel cellDistribution``. When given, only the cells
       listed in its ``cells`` file are computed.
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``writeSpectra``
     - ``all`` / ``none`` / labelList
     - ``all``
     - Cells whose radiance spectra are written
       (``spectra_<window>.bin``); a list gives cell indices k.
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``writeCoefficients``
     - bool
     - ``false``
     - Also write :math:`j_\lambda` and :math:`\kappa_\lambda` of the
       ``writeSpectra`` cells (``emission_<window>.bin``,
       ``absorption_<window>.bin``).
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``writeDistributions``
     - ``all`` / ``none`` / labelList
     - ``none``
     - Cells whose level populations are written to
       ``distribution/cell<k>`` in the ``dsmcRadiationDistribution``
       format (17 digits), so that a Boltzmann profile can be replayed
       through ``cellDistribution``.
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``instrument``
     - dict
     - none
     - EAST measurement model: one sub-dictionary per camera
       (`instrument cameras`_). Entries that are not dictionaries are
       ignored.
     - ``shockTube1D.C``

Cell numbering k: the profile cells sorted by x (0 to N-1), or the index k
of the distribution files when ``distribution`` is given.

~~~~~~~~~~~

``profiles/shockTubeProfile/shockTubeProfile.C``. The cells are sorted by
increasing x after reading; negative values of any field are set to 0.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``source``
     - word
     - required

     - ``shockTubeProfile.C``
   * - ``file``
     - fileName
     - required (macroFile)

       directory when not absolute.
     - ``shockTubeProfile.C``
   * - ``electronNumberDensity``
     - scalar [m\ :sup:`-3`]

     - ``shockTubeProfile.C``

     - dict
     - required (dsmcCase)

       sub-dictionary, uses the defaults.
     - ``shockTubeProfile.C``
   * - ``electrons``
     - dict
     - required (dsmcCase)

``profile/species/<s>`` (dsmcCase): names of the ``volScalarField`` read
for each quantity. The defaults are the names written by ``dsmcVolFields``;
dsmcFoam writes one internal temperature per species (the temperature
whose Boltzmann distribution over the rovibronic ladder has the sampled
mean internal energy), so the three internal temperatures default to the
same field.

   * - Keyword
     - Default field
     - Quantity
   * - ``numberDensity``
     - ``rhoN_<s>``
     - number density [m\ :sup:`-3`]
   * - ``translationalTemperature``
     - ``translationalT_<s>``
     - translational temperature [K]
   * - ``rotationalTemperature``
     - ``internalT_<s>``
     - temperature of the rotational factor of the re-expansion [K]
   * - ``vibrationalTemperature``
     - ``internalT_<s>``
     - temperature of the vibrational factor of the re-expansion [K]
   * - ``electronicTemperature``
     - ``internalT_<s>``
     - temperature of the electronic factor of the re-expansion [K]

Example (``tutorials/shockTube1D/fromDsmcCase``):

.. code-block:: text

   profile
   {
       source      dsmcCase;
       case        "../dsmcCase";
       time        latestTime;
       direction   x;
       species
       {
           N2 { rotationalTemperature translationalT_N2; }
           N  { }
           O  { }
       }
       electrons
       {
           numberDensity   1e+21;      // [m^-3] or a field name
           temperature     10000;      // [K] or a field name
       }
   }

Macro file
~~~~~~~~~~

A comma-separated text file, one row per cell; lines starting with ``#``
are comments; the first other line names the columns, in any order:

.. code-block:: text

   # synthetic post-shock profile (x [m]; n [m^-3], T [K])
   x,n_e,T_e,n_N2,Ttrans_N2,Telec_N2,Trot_N2,Tvib_N2,n_N,Ttrans_N,Telec_N
   -0.00345,1e+21,10000.0,2.56e+21,300.0,300.0,300.0,300.0,1e15,300.0,300.0

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 20 55

   * - Column
     - Required
     - Meaning
   * - ``x``
     - yes
     - position along the tube [m]
   * - ``n_e``, ``T_e``
     - no
     - electrons; else ``electronNumberDensity``/``electronTemperature``
       of ``profile``
   * - ``n_<s>``, ``Ttrans_<s>``
     - yes, per species
     - number density [m\ :sup:`-3`], translational temperature [K]
   * - ``Trot_<s>``, ``Tvib_<s>``, ``Telec_<s>``
     - no
     - 0 when absent; a missing ``Telec_<s>`` is reported (no electronic
       excitation)

A row with another number of values than the header is a FatalIOError.

``distribution``
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``directory``
     - fileName
     - required
     - A ``dsmcRadiationDistribution`` output directory,
       ``.../postProcessing/dsmcRadiationDistribution/<name>/<time>``,
       holding ``cells`` and ``cell<k>``. Expanded; relative to the case
       directory when not absolute.
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``direction``
     - word
     - ``x``
     - Component of the cell centres of the ``cells`` file matched to the
       profile positions: ``x``, ``y`` or ``z``.
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``matchTolerance``
     - scalar [-]
     - 1e-3
     - Each distribution cell is matched to the nearest profile cell; the
       distance must be below ``matchTolerance`` times the smallest
       profile cell spacing (FatalIOError otherwise).
     - ``shockTube1D.C``

The ``cells`` file is read line by line (``#`` comments skipped): cell
index k, then the centre ``x y z``; further columns are ignored. An empty
list is a FatalIOError.

Instrument cameras
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

``shockTube1DCoeffs/instrument/<camera>``: the EAST measurement model of
one camera, applied to the raw radiance field :math:`I(x,\lambda)` of the
cells sorted by x. The entries are read partly by ``shockTube1D``
(``shockTube1D.C``) and partly by ``shockTubeInstrument``
(``instrument/shockTubeInstrument/shockTubeInstrument.C``). Theory in
:doc:`/theory/instrument`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``window``
     - word
     - the only window
     - Window measured by the camera; required when there is more than
       one window.
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``wavelengthRange``
     - (a b) [nm]
     - whole window
     - Sub-range of the window; must hold at least two grid points.
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``spectralResponse``
     - dict
     - identity
     - Instrument line shape (ILS): an `instrument function`_, convolution
       in wavelength.
     - ``shockTubeInstrument.C``
   * - ``spatialResponse``
     - dict
     - identity
     - Spatial resolution function (SRF): an instrument function,
       convolution in x (use ``composite`` for optics, camera and shock
       motion).
     - ``shockTubeInstrument.C``
   * - ``bandUnit``
     - word
     - ``nm``
     - Unit of the camera bands: ``m``, ``um`` or ``nm``.
     - ``shockTubeInstrument.C``
   * - ``bands``
     - dict
     - none
     - Measured bands ``name (min max);`` [bandUnit], max > min.
     - ``shockTubeInstrument.C``
   * - ``shockFront``
     - dict
     - empty
     - Shock-front criterion (`shockFront`_).
     - both
   * - ``distanceUnit``
     - word
     - ``m``
     - Unit of the distances of this camera (``position``,
       ``metricHalfWidth``, ``spatialBinWidth``, ``spectra``): ``m``,
       ``cm`` or ``mm``.
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``metricHalfWidth``
     - scalar [distanceUnit]
     - 0.02 m
     - Half width :math:`h` of the nonequilibrium metric
       :math:`(1/D)\int_{x_0-h}^{x_0+h}(\cdot)\,dx`, in ``distanceUnit``.
       Absent: 2 cm (0.02 m, the EAST definition) whatever
       ``distanceUnit``. The interval is clipped to the profile
       (warning).
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``spatialBinWidth``
     - scalar [distanceUnit]
     - none
     - Optional uniform x bins: the raw radiance is averaged over bins of
       this width (weighted by the cell widths) before the measurement.
       Must be positive.
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``spectra``
     - dict
     - none
     - Measured spectra (`spectra`_).
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``writeField``
     - bool
     - ``false``
     - Write the whole measured field
       (``instrument_<camera>_field.bin`` and ``.info``).
     - ``shockTube1D.C``

shockFront
^^^^^^^^^^

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``criterion``
     - word
     - ``peakRadiance``
     - ``peakRadiance`` (parabola-refined maximum), ``maxGradient``
       (steepest rise from the upstream side) or ``riseFraction``. Any
       other value: FatalIOError.
     - ``shockTubeInstrument.C``
   * - ``band``
     - word
     - first camera band
     - Camera band whose measured profile locates the front; must be one
       of ``bands``.
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``position``
     - scalar [distanceUnit]
     - none
     - Fixed front position :math:`x_0` instead of a criterion. Without
       it, the camera needs at least one band.
     - ``shockTube1D.C``
   * - ``upstreamSide``
     - word
     - ``low``
     - ``low``: x increases from the free stream into the shocked gas;
       ``high``: the opposite; any other word is a FatalIOError. Sets the
       sign of the distance behind the shock.
     - both
   * - ``fraction``
     - scalar
     - 0.5
     - ``riseFraction``: the front is where the profile crosses
       :math:`f_\mathrm{pre}+\mathrm{fraction}\,(f_\mathrm{post}-f_\mathrm{pre})`.
     - ``shockTubeInstrument.C``
   * - ``preValue``, ``postValue``
     - scalar
     - end values
     - ``riseFraction``: fixed pre- and post-shock values instead of the
       profile end values.
     - ``shockTubeInstrument.C``

spectra
^^^^^^^

Each sub-dictionary of ``spectra`` is one measured spectrum, a column of
``instrument_<camera>_spectra.csv`` [W cm\ :sup:`-2` sr\ :sup:`-1`
µm\ :sup:`-1`]. Exactly one of:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 25 50

   * - Entry
     - Type
     - Spectrum
   * - ``at``
     - ``peak`` or scalar [distanceUnit]
     - At a distance d behind the front (linear interpolation in x between
       cells), or at the parabola-refined peak of the front band (needs a
       front band).
   * - ``average``
     - (d1 d2) [distanceUnit]
     - Mean over the distances d1 to d2 behind the front.
   * - ``metric``
     - scalar h [distanceUnit]
     - Spectral nonequilibrium metric
       :math:`(1/D)\int_{x_0-h}^{x_0+h}I\,dx`.

A requested position outside the profile is a FatalIOError (for
``average`` and ``metric`` a partly outside range is clipped with a
warning). A sub-dictionary with none of the three is a FatalIOError.

Example (one camera of ``tutorials/EAST/east5029``):

.. code-block:: c++

   instrument
   {
       VUV
       {
           window          VUV;
           spectralResponse
           {
               type            eastLineShape;
               lengthUnit      nm;
               form            sqrtVoigt;
               halfWidthGauss  0.084;
               halfWidthLorentz 0.0025;
               cutoff          56.1;       // camera width: the line shape spans the detector
           }
           spatialResponse
           {
               type            composite;
               lengthUnit      cm;
               functions
               {
                   optics  { type trapezoid; baseWidth 0.041; topWidth 0; }
                   camera  { type eastLineShape; form sqrtVoigt;
                             halfWidthGauss 0.026; halfWidthLorentz 0.063; cutoff 10; }
                   motion  { type topHat; width 0.514; }
               }
           }
           bandUnit        nm;
           bands           { VUV_EAST (162 218); }
           shockFront      { criterion peakRadiance; band VUV_EAST; upstreamSide low; }
           distanceUnit    cm;
           metricHalfWidth 2;
           spatialBinWidth 0.005;
           spectra
           {
               nonequilibrium  { at peak; }
               metric          { metric 2; }
               equilibrium     { average (3 4.5); }
           }
       }
   }

Instrument function
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Every ``spectralResponse``, ``spatialResponse``, every member of a
``composite`` and every ``function`` of the test application is an
``instrumentFunction``, run-time selected by ``type``
(``instrument/instrumentFunctions``). The coefficients sit directly in the
dictionary (no ``Coeffs`` sub-dictionary). Entries of the base class
(``basic/instrumentFunction/instrumentFunction.C``):

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 12 14 38 16

   * - Keyword
     - Type
     - Default
     - Meaning
     - Source
   * - ``type``
     - word
     - required
     - ``gaussian``, ``topHat``, ``trapezoid``, ``voigt``,
       ``eastLineShape``, ``tabulated`` or ``composite``.
     - ``instrumentFunctionNew.C``
   * - ``lengthUnit``
     - word
     - ``m``
     - Unit of every width of the function: ``m``, ``cm``, ``mm``, ``um``
       or ``nm``.
     - ``instrumentFunction.C``
   * - ``conserveAtEdges``
     - bool
     - ``true``
     - ``true``: the part of a kernel that falls outside the window is
       given back to the window, so the trapezoid integral is conserved to
       round-off. ``false``: it is lost, as on a detector of finite size.
     - ``instrumentFunction.C``
   * - ``convolution``
     - word
     - ``auto``
     - Sum over the sources on a uniform grid: ``direct``, ``fft``, or
       ``auto`` (FFT when the kernel stencil is wider than the grid and the
       grid has at least 4096 points). Same weights and normalisation; the
       two differ by round-off only.
     - ``instrumentFunction.C``

Coefficients of each type (lengths in ``lengthUnit``):

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 16 22 14 48

   * - type
     - Keyword
     - Default
     - Meaning
   * - ``gaussian``
     - ``fwhm`` or ``sigma``
     - one required
     - Full width at half maximum, or the standard deviation (``sigma``
       wins if both are given); must be positive.
   * -
     - ``cutoff``
     - 10
     - Kernel cut at :math:`|s|=\mathrm{cutoff}\,\sigma`.
   * - ``topHat``
     - ``width``
     - see next
     - Full width :math:`w`.
   * -
     - ``shockSpeed``, ``gateTime``
     - required without ``width``
     - :math:`w=U_s t_\mathrm{gate}` [m s\ :sup:`-1`] × [s], always SI
       (smear of the shock during the camera gate).
   * - ``trapezoid``
     - ``baseWidth``
     - required
     - Bottom width :math:`b`.
   * -
     - ``topWidth``
     - 0
     - Top width :math:`t`, :math:`0\le t\le b` (0: triangle).
   * - ``voigt``
     - ``fwhmGauss``, ``fwhmLorentz``
     - required
     - FWHM of the Gaussian and Lorentzian parts (0 gives the other pure
       profile).
   * -
     - ``form``
     - ``exact``
     - ``exact`` (tabulated convolution) or ``pseudoVoigt`` (Thompson, Cox
       and Hastings 1987).
   * -
     - ``cutoff``
     - 100
     - Wings cut at :math:`|s|=\mathrm{cutoff}\,f_V` (:math:`f_V` the
       Olivero-Longbothum width).
   * -
     - ``resolution``
     - 100
     - Table nodes per :math:`f_V` (``exact``).
   * - ``eastLineShape``
     - ``form``
     - required
     - ``sqrtVoigt`` (ILS1 of Brandis and Cruden 2017) or
       ``gaussLorentz`` (ILS2).
   * -
     - ``halfWidthGauss``, ``halfWidthLorentz``
     - required
     - Half widths at half maximum :math:`w_g`, :math:`w_l` (both
       :math:`\ge 0`, not both zero).
   * -
     - ``cutoff``
     - required
     - Half range :math:`R` of the normalisation, a length (the
       ``sqrtVoigt`` wings decay as :math:`1/|s|`, so the result depends on
       it); must be positive.
   * -
     - ``ratio``
     - 0
     - :math:`a\in[0,1]` of ``gaussLorentz``.
   * -
     - ``resolution``
     - 100
     - Table nodes per Voigt FWHM (``sqrtVoigt``).
   * - ``tabulated``
     - ``file``
     - required
     - Two-column ASCII file: offset (in ``lengthUnit``) and response
       (:math:`\ge 0`, any scale); ``#`` comments. Expanded; relative to
       the case (the working directory for Test-radiationInstrument).
   * -
     - ``centre``
     - 0
     - Offset subtracted from the file offsets.
   * -
     - ``centreFirstMoment``
     - ``false``
     - Put the centroid at 0 instead of using ``centre``.
   * - ``composite``
     - ``functions``
     - required
     - Sub-dictionaries, each an instrument function (any type, composite
       included); they inherit ``lengthUnit`` unless they set their own.
   * -
     - ``resolution``
     - 200
     - Table step :math:`h` = smallest component width / resolution.
   * -
     - ``maxNodes``
     - 400001
     - Cap on the number of table nodes.

Test application dictionary
---------------------------

``Test-radiationInstrument``
(``applications/test/radiationInstrument/Test-radiationInstrument.C``)
reads the dictionary named by ``-dict`` (default
``system/instrumentTests``). Every sub-dictionary except ``FoamFile`` is a
test, run in order:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 14 40 46

   * - ``kind``
     - Entries
     - Output
   * - ``convolve``
     - ``function {...}``, ``input`` (two columns: y [m], f), ``output``
     - y, f, g = K ⊗ f; the trapezoid integrals of f and g
   * - ``kernel``
     - ``function {...}``, ``nodes (min max n)`` [m] (n ≥ 2), ``output``
     - s, pdf(s), cdf(s)
   * - ``band``
     - ``input`` (:math:`\lambda` [m], I), ``bands { name (min max); }``
       [nm], ``output``
     - name, bounds, integral
   * - ``front``
     - ``input`` (x [m], f), ``output``
     - front location by peakRadiance, maxGradient and riseFraction (0.5,
       upstream low, end values)
   * - ``measure``
     - ``instrument {...}`` (a camera as above, without the shockTube1D
       entries), ``input`` (first line nx nl; then nl wavelengths, nx
       positions, nx × nl values), ``output``, ``bandOutput``
     - the measured field (same format) and the band profiles

Input files with fewer than two rows or a line without two numbers are
fatal. See :doc:`tests`.

Removed entries
---------------

The following entries and values were removed on 2026-11-14
(``docs/RADIATION_RESTRUCTURE.md`` section 6). A case that still gives one
stops with a FatalIOError naming the behaviour that is kept; delete the
entry.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 28 22 50

   * - Entry
     - Where
     - Behaviour kept
   * - ``windowEdge``
     - species dict or any enclosing dict
     - every grid point receives the line-profile integral over its own
       bin, also at the window edges
   * - ``subBinLines``
     - species dict or any enclosing dict
     - same: lines narrower than a bin are integrated over their bin
   * - ``starkDensity``
     - species dict or any enclosing dict
     - electron-impact Stark width with :math:`n_e` and :math:`T_e` of
       the cell
   * - ``slabFormula``
     - ``shockTube1DCoeffs``
     - :math:`I=-(j/\kappa)\,\mathrm{expm1}(-\kappa D)`
   * - ``continuum piclas``
     - top level or species
     - ``continuum none`` or ``physical``
   * - ``type piclasInstrBroadening``
     - instrument function
     - ``type trapezoid`` (unit-area trapezoid of ``baseWidth`` and
       ``topWidth``)
