EAST radiance validation
========================

The November 2026 benchmark compares a corrected radiationFoam calculation
with the calibrated EAST measurements. The uniform equilibrium columns test
the spectrum and instrument response at a prescribed gas state. A separate
calculation uses the spatially varying DSMC state of shot 50-29.

Equilibrium columns
-------------------

The 50 decks comprise 26 benchmark columns, four full camera spectra and
20 species diagnostics. The five windows contain 28 shot–camera pairs from
24 different shots at 0.2 Torr. Each entry below is the geometric mean of
the predicted radiance divided by the measured radiance over the shots in
that window. The range gives the smallest and largest corrected ratios.
The nominal 170–180 nm band ends at 178 nm for the Test 57 VUV camera.

The baseline reproduced all 236 archived output files from revision
``f151155``. Both builds used the same equilibrium states and detector
response. The corrected build uses the published Whiting wing term, a
consistent integration grid and molecular collision-partner masses in
atomic mass units. See :doc:`/theory/line_shapes`.

For the equilibrium spectra, integrating on common camera coverage gives
3.288 versus 4.814 W cm\ :sup:`−2` sr\ :sup:`−1` for shot 50-29
(162–218 nm), and 13.478 versus 17.750 for shot 57-16 (122–178 nm).
In the 159.5–164 nm gap of shot 57-16, the predicted mean spectral radiance
changes from 28.15 to 25.70, against 29.78 W cm\ :sup:`−2` sr\ :sup:`−1`
µm\ :sup:`−1` measured. The correction reduces the mean excess at 170–180 nm
and increases the deficit in the deep VUV.

.. _east57-vuv-peaks:

Shot 57-16 VUV peaks
--------------------

The Test 57 fill contained 78.08 mol% N₂, 20.95 mol% O₂, 0.93 mol% Ar and
0.04 mol% CO₂. Each incident shock was solved twice with NASA CEA 3.3.4, using
the reported fill and a CO₂-free control with the other gases in the same
proportions. Both predictions include Ar and Ar⁺ spectroscopy. For shot 50-29,
the nominal fill was 79 mol% N₂ and 21 mol% O₂. Its 400 ppm CO₂ calculation is
a sensitivity case. The spatially resolved DSMC comparison uses nominal Test 50 air.

The equilibrium chemistry includes C, CO, CO₂, C₂, CN and their singly charged
positive ions. At 10,414 K in shot 57-16, C and C⁺ account for 99.95% of
the carbon nuclei. Both atomic carbon stages and the CO, CO⁺, C₂ and CN
bands contribute to the emission and absorption of the same 10.16 cm column.
The calculation applies the detector response after solving radiative transfer.

The reported fill recovers 95% of the area of the first feature and 205% of
the second. At 157.92 nm, EAST measures 113 W cm⁻² sr⁻¹ µm⁻¹. The CO₂-free
and reported-fill calculations give 29 and 37 in the same units. Switching
off the four carbon molecular radiators at fixed composition changes the latter
by 0.01. The carrier of this weaker maximum remains unassigned. The separate
carbon-slab diagnostic is archived in the :download:`earlier comparison
<../../../verification/eastSweep/results/carbon_check.csv>`.

CO₂, CO₂⁺, C₂⁺ and CN⁺ enter the chemistry. Their high-temperature electronic
opacities are unresolved. For the uniform equilibrium state of 57-16, an
electronic oscillator-strength bound places the combined emitted radiance of
CO₂, CO₂⁺ and C₂⁺ below 0.0000021 W cm⁻² sr⁻¹ over the VUV detector.
The corresponding upper bound for CN⁺ is 0.0093 W cm⁻² sr⁻¹.

For 50-29, adding 400 ppm CO₂ raises the 162–218 nm VUV integral from
3.278 to 4.147 W cm⁻² sr⁻¹, compared with 4.814 measured. Over 339–471 nm
the Blue camera changes from 1.666 to 1.669 W cm⁻² sr⁻¹, compared with 2.445 measured.

Extended Tests 50 and 57
------------------------

An additional 104 equilibrium columns cover 44 shots from Tests 50 and 57.
They use the recorded fill pressure and shock speed for each shot and the
representative camera responses used in the campaign analysis. Integrating
the investigators’ released equilibrium spectra yields 127 measured band
comparisons. The VUV spectra of 50-92 and 50-99 contain missing pixels
in the 170–178 nm window, so their measured integrals are unavailable.

For the 26 shots at or above 10 km/s with complete 170–178 nm measurements,
a least-squares fit of log(predicted/measured) to shock speed, with a
separate intercept for each of four calibration periods, gives a slope
of 0.310 ± 0.076 per km/s. Omitting the three shots previously flagged in
the campaign analysis (50-32, 57-21 and 57-43) gives 0.098 ± 0.053 per km/s
over 23 shots. These are regression standard errors. The inferred dependence
on speed is sensitive to shot selection.

DSMC production comparison for shot 50-29
-----------------------------------------

The shock speed was 10.29 km/s in a 79% nitrogen and 21% oxygen mixture
at 0.2 Torr. dsmcFoam+ accumulated ten 10 µs blocks from 150 to 250 µs.
radiationFoam evaluated every tenth cell in the radiation zone (958 of
9,580 cells), using the time-averaged sampled populations and a second
reconstruction from the internal temperatures. Both calculations used
the same DSMC realisation, species densities, slab geometry and detector
response. Replaying the Boltzmann populations in six cells reproduced
the emission coefficient byte for byte in both windows.

The table gives band radiances in W cm\ :sup:`−2` sr\ :sup:`−1`.
The downstream values average the profile from 3 to 4.5 cm. The near-front
values integrate it from -0.576 to 2.000 cm
and divide by the 10.16 cm tube diameter. The computed origin is the density
half-rise. Each measurement uses the half-rise of its smoothed camera-band
radiance. The upstream limit is the first sampled DSMC cell. Brandis and
Cruden integrate a full four centimetres about a radiance peak, or the
inflection point for a profile without overshoot. The shorter interval here
permits a direct comparison of the three profiles on their shared coverage.

Downstream, the sampled populations give 0.87 of the measured VUV radiance and 0.74 of the measured UV/Vis radiance. The Boltzmann reconstruction gives 1.06 and 0.89, respectively. Over the shared near-front interval, the sampled and Boltzmann VUV integrals are 0.85 and 2.52 times EAST. In the UV/Vis those ratios are 1.04 and 1.45. At the 17 downstream diagnostic positions, the median sampled-to-Boltzmann population ratio is 1.04 for the upper term of the 174.3 nm nitrogen multiplet and 0.54 for the B state of the nitrogen molecular ion. The sampled C state of molecular nitrogen is empty at all 17 positions and the A state of nitric oxide is empty at 16. The empty groups limit what this single realisation can establish about the least populated emitters.

Verification
------------

All 375 comparisons between the expanded and short inputs passed. The
suite reproduced 26 reference files. The instrument passed 53 checks.
The largest relative difference from the independent atomic-line calculation
across 16 ion cases was 1.54 × 10\ :sup:`−10` at points above 0.1% of
the peak. An independent integration of the six production bands
reproduced all 54 spatial statistics at the precision of the archived tables.
